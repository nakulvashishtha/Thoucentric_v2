# Demo: a 30-minute boardroom slot

**Before you start (T-5 min):** open the app address so the free server wakes up, type the passcode, press **Settings > Run checks** (all green). Have a rehearsed live case already at step 12, and a fresh browser tab on the home page.

## Part 1: the tool on a sample (about 10 minutes, demo data, said openly)
1. **Home.** Read the one line: the tool does the searching, sorting and maths; you check what matters and make the call. Click **Try a sample > Credit card launch**. Point out the "Demo data mode" banner.
2. **Describe the ask.** The client's words are kept as written; files already received are used first; "Never send these" holds private numbers. Click **Check the ask**.
3. **Confirm the question.** The belief (30% growth) becomes a claim to test, not a fact. Click **Confirm question**.
4. **Set the targets.** Open H1: the benchmark was fetched before any evidence was read. Show **Critical**. Tick the box, **Lock targets**: the goalposts can't move.
5. **Skip ahead:** Settings > **Skip to step** 7. Say: it replays the sample's answers through the same checks.
6. **Plan the research** (stepper, step 4): the client request asks only for gaps; **Details** shows the client's name removed from every search.
7. **Review the evidence.** The progress chips; one item opened: quote highlighted in its source, the calculation to confirm, every check with its threshold. The items that passed the quality check still show their source; the quick double-check keeps the check honest. **Run tests** stays locked until every call is made.
8. **Results.** Point at the target-versus-evidence bar on H1: both separate sources sit below 20%. Not supported, Strong.
9. **Fill the gaps.** Only H2 goes back, with a narrower question. **Use the sample reply**, accept it, **Test again**: Supported, Fair (client data caps confidence).
10. **The answer.** Not achievable, because a critical idea failed. Every sentence cites its evidence; the rejected blog stays on record.
11. **What if...** Move "Months needed to build" to 9: H4 flips. "Locked plan unchanged."
12. **Your conclusion.** The box is empty. The tool never writes it. Show **Print or save PDF**.

## Part 2: one live case (about 10 minutes)
- Open the rehearsed live case at step 12 and walk back through steps 4, 7 and 8 with the stepper.
- In step 6, open **Details** and read the line on how many figures could not be found word for word in their source (the drop rate of unverified quotes).
- Show **Settings > Spending**: what this case cost.

## Part 3: questions (about 10 minutes)
- **History** (top bar) shows every action, tagged You, Calculated or AI draft, with **Export CSV**.
- Sample 2 (new hospital, a different industry) runs on the same code: proof the tool is general.

**If the search service or the model is slow:** say so, press **Use demo data** on a sample case (or open Sample 1), and carry on. Never pretend demo data is live.
